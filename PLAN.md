# Neblima roadmap

The first release focuses on retrospective hourly PM2.5 forecasting for Lima.
It combines a verified local TimeF export, baseline and temporal-model
comparisons, and a public dashboard. The [evaluation guide](docs/evaluation.md)
records what was measured.

The [work plan (Spanish)](tasks/plan.md) tracks the sequence of data,
evaluation, and presentation work with links to its evidence. Use the
[issue tracker](https://github.com/keneth-arias/Neblima/issues) for current
owners, dependencies, and status. The [initial plan
(Spanish)](docs/initial-plan.md) preserves the original scope and proposed
milestones.
